If you would like to purchase a piece, please contact Studio AKASAKA by email or Instagram direct message, stating the product number of the work you are interested in.

We will check its availability and get back to you.

For those who are able to collect the piece directly from Studio AKASAKA in Berlin, we would be pleased to meet you in person and hand the work to you. Shipping is also available for those living farther away.

If you require shipping, please provide your delivery address before making payment. We will confirm the shipping cost and send you the total amount, including the price of the piece, by email.

Payment is generally made in cash or by bank transfer. If you prefer to pay by credit card, we can send you a secure Stripe payment link.

You are also welcome to contact us if you would like to see the piece in person before purchasing. Visits to the studio are by appointment, so please let us know a few dates and times that would be convenient for you.
